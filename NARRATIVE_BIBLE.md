# NARRATIVE BIBLE
**Kettleford** — small riverside town, old mill, rain more often than not. The council has kept its one emergency station on a shoestring; you're sent to run it.
**Main thread:** trees and infrastructure keep failing a little too conveniently. Priya finds a drill hole in the Alder Lane oak (day 3+). Someone wants parts of Kettleford condemned (seeds the riverside regeneration conflict).
**Crew**
- *Maren Holt* — veteran captain. Brilliant, dry, distrusts newcomers after a past chief cut corners. Trust rises with safe scenes, drops with unsafe cuts. Off duty: allotment, rescue greyhound.
- *Theo Vance* — rookie. Brave, funny, impulsive; eats evidence (pastries). Wants to be trusted with real jobs; delegation raises his trust.
- *Priya Nair* — mechanic. Keeps the station running on scrap; weighing a job offer from a city depot. Upgrades go through her.
- *Player* — customisable station leader.
**Residents:** *Rosa Mendes* (sharp, generous, grandson Tomás's recital matters), *Tomás Mendes* (9, thinks you're a captain), *Arthur Penhallow* (retired bandleader, trombone, insists he's fine).
**Implemented beats:** intro; first-arrival delegation hint; live-wire warning; unsafe-cut rebuke; Rosa/Arthur thanks or roof loss; safe-scene praise; next-day pastries; tarp on Alder Lane; roof patched; drilled oak.
**Tone rules:** warmth over tragedy; humour from character, not mockery; consequences shown, not lectured; every resident remembers.
