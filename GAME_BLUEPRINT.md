# GAME BLUEPRINT
**Promise:** Every rescue leaves a story.
**Loop:** Prepare → call → assess (briefing: severity, weather, hints) → respond (drive or quick dispatch) → hands-on work + delegation → outcome & consequences → return → crew/community/board → end shift.
**Session shape:** routine call 2–5 min (time limit 5:00); story beats between calls; one call per shift in the slice.
**Meaningful choices (implemented):** drive vs quick dispatch; cone first vs cut first (speed vs safety: payout bonus, captain trust, story); delegate to Theo vs do it yourself; radio the utility before cutting; spend money on equipment vs repairing a resident's roof.
**Hands-on families:** 1) Debris removal (done: chainsaw bind timing, carrying, stacking). 2) Flood pumping (next).
**Failure:** timer or abandon → roof damage persists, road closed overnight, relationship dips, repair project unlocks.
**Progression:** funds → equipment (chainsaw service: faster, less binding) and community projects; crew xp/trust/morale.
**Monetisation stance:** free-to-start, cosmetics/story chapters later; no ads, no timers, never monetise injury or delay.
